Frequently Asked Questions

What imaging services does Ibn Sina Hospital offer?
We offer a full range of diagnostic imaging services including MRI, CT scan, digital X-ray, ultrasound, Doppler studies, and mammography. Our advanced equipment ensures high-quality images for accurate diagnosis.
Do I need a referral for a radiology test?
Yes, most radiology tests require a referral from a doctor. However, we also accept walk-in patients for general X-rays and ultrasounds with a valid prescription. For advanced imaging like MRI and CT, a referral is mandatory.
How long does it take to get radiology results?
Preliminary results are usually available within 24 hours. Urgent cases are prioritized and reported immediately. Our radiologists provide detailed reports to your referring physician promptly.
Is radiology safe during pregnancy?
Ultrasound is safe during pregnancy and commonly used. X-rays and CT scans are generally avoided unless absolutely necessary. Our team takes extra precautions to minimize exposure and only perform these when the benefit outweighs the risk.
Do you offer contrast-enhanced scans?
Yes, we offer contrast-enhanced CT and MRI scans to better visualize blood vessels, tumors, and other structures. Our staff will explain the procedure and any necessary preparation.
