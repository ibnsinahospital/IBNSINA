Frequently Asked Questions

What are the common signs of labour I should watch for?
Common signs include regular contractions, water breaking (rupture of membranes), back pain, and a bloody show. If you experience any of these, especially after 37 weeks, contact our labour ward immediately at 9622552553.
When should I schedule my first prenatal appointment?
We recommend scheduling your first prenatal visit as soon as you suspect you are pregnant or within the first 8–10 weeks. Early care ensures proper monitoring of your baby's development and your health.
What gynaecological surgeries are available at Ibn Sina Hospital?
We offer a full range of gynaecological surgeries including laparoscopic hysterectomy, myomectomy, ovarian cyst removal, endometriosis excision, and C-sections. Our surgeons use minimally invasive techniques for faster recovery.
Do you manage high-risk pregnancies at Ibn Sina Hospital?
Yes, our gynaecology department is equipped to manage high-risk pregnancies, including those with gestational diabetes, pre-eclampsia, multiple gestations, and previous C-sections. Our consultant-led team provides 24/7 monitoring and emergency care.
What facilities are available for childbirth and maternity care?
We provide state-of-the-art labour and delivery suites, a dedicated maternity ward, NICU for newborns, 24/7 blood bank, pharmacy, and round-the-clock emergency obstetric care. Our team ensures a safe and comfortable birthing experience.
