What to Expect

1. Consultation

Your journey begins with a private, unhurried conversation with one of our gynaecologists. We listen carefully to your symptoms, medical history, and any concerns you may have – whether you are planning a pregnancy, managing a chronic condition, or simply due for a routine check-up.

2. Diagnosis

Based on your consultation, we may recommend diagnostic tests such as pelvic ultrasound, blood work, Pap smears, or advanced imaging. All investigations are performed on-site at Ibn Sina Hospital, so you get quick results without travelling to multiple centres.

3. Treatment

Your treatment plan is tailored to you. It may include lifestyle advice, medication, minor procedures, or advanced gynaecological surgery – including minimally invasive (laparoscopic) options that reduce pain and speed up recovery. Our surgeons are highly skilled in both routine and complex operations.

4. Follow-up

Care does not end when you leave the hospital. We schedule regular follow-up appointments to monitor your progress, adjust treatments if needed, and provide ongoing support. We are with you every step of the way, from initial diagnosis to full recovery.

Facilities

  • Fully equipped labour and delivery suites with comfortable birthing beds
  • Advanced laparoscopic (minimally invasive) surgical unit for gynaecological procedures
  • High-dependency obstetric beds for managing high-risk pregnancies
  • Continuous foetal monitoring systems (CTG) for safe labour management
  • In-house ultrasound and diagnostic imaging services
  • 24/7 blood bank and emergency obstetric care
  • Private consultation rooms for confidential discussions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment with a gynaecologist at Ibn Sina Hospital?

You can book an appointment by calling our reception desk directly, visiting the hospital in person, or using our online appointment portal. Our friendly staff will help you find a convenient time with the doctor of your choice.

Do you accept health insurance and cashless claims?

Yes, we accept most major health insurance policies and offer cashless treatment for eligible patients. Please bring your insurance card and valid ID at the time of admission, and our billing team will assist you with the paperwork.

Are female gynaecologists available at the hospital?

Absolutely. We have several highly qualified female gynaecologists – including Dr. Snober Malik, Dr. Farhat Tabbsum, and Dr. Madhu Sayal – who provide personal and sensitive care for women who prefer a female doctor.

Do you offer both normal delivery and C-section options?

Yes. Our obstetricians support natural vaginal deliveries with pain relief options, and we also perform planned and emergency caesarean sections whenever medically indicated. We always prioritise the safety of both mother and baby.

What should I do if I have a gynaecological emergency after hours?

Ibn Sina Hospital has a 24/7 emergency department with on-call gynaecologists available at all times. If you experience severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, or any concerning symptoms during pregnancy, come to the hospital immediately or call our emergency helpline – we are always ready to help.
