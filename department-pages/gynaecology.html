Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer high-risk pregnancy care for patients from Srinagar?

Yes, our gynaecologists are experienced in managing high-risk pregnancies, including diabetes, hypertension, and multiple pregnancies, with round-the-clock emergency support.

Are laparoscopic surgeries available for fibroids and ovarian cysts?

Absolutely. We perform advanced laparoscopic procedures that ensure smaller incisions, less pain, and faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery.

Can I get a routine cervical cancer screening in Budgam?

Yes, we offer Pap smears, HPV testing, and colposcopy services with same-day reporting.

Do you have female gynaecologists available?

Yes, our team includes both male and female gynaecologists. You can request a female doctor when booking your appointment.

Is cashless insurance accepted for gynaecological treatments?

We accept all major cashless insurance plans. Our billing team will assist you with pre-authorization and claim processing.
