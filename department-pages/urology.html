Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does a urologist treat?
Urologists treat conditions of the urinary tract in both men and women, including kidney stones, urinary tract infections, bladder issues, prostate disorders, male infertility, erectile dysfunction, and urological cancers (kidney, bladder, prostate).
What are the symptoms of kidney stones?
Common symptoms include severe pain in the back, side, or lower abdomen, pain during urination, blood in urine, nausea, vomiting, and frequent urination. If you experience these, consult our urologist immediately.
Do you offer minimally invasive urological surgeries?
Yes, we offer advanced minimally invasive procedures including laparoscopic urological surgery, laser stone treatment (URS, RIRS), TURP for prostate, and endoscopic procedures. These techniques ensure faster recovery and reduced discomfort.
What is the treatment for enlarged prostate (BPH)?
Treatment options include medications (alpha-blockers, 5-alpha reductase inhibitors), minimally invasive procedures (TURP, laser therapy), and in some cases, surgery. Our urologist will recommend the best approach based on your condition and age.
Do you treat male infertility and erectile dysfunction?
Yes, our urology department provides comprehensive evaluation and treatment for male infertility, erectile dysfunction, and other sexual health issues. We offer medical management, counselling, and surgical options where necessary.
