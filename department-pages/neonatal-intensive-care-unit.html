Our NICU Care Team

Our Neonatal Intensive Care Unit is staffed 24/7 by experienced pediatricians and specially trained neonatal nursing staff, working under consultant supervision to monitor and care for premature and critically ill newborns around the clock. Our wider team of pediatric and general medicine consultants is available for referrals and multidisciplinary support whenever a newborn needs it.

What to Expect – Your Baby's Care Journey

1

Immediate Newborn Assessment

Every newborn requiring extra support is assessed immediately after birth or on referral, with rapid decisions on the level of care needed.

2

Continuous Monitoring

Babies are monitored around the clock for heart rate, oxygen levels, temperature, and weight gain using dedicated neonatal equipment.

3

Specialised Newborn Treatment

From respiratory support to phototherapy for jaundice and infection management, care is tailored to each baby's specific needs.

4

Family Support & Discharge Planning

We keep parents informed throughout the stay and guide families on feeding, care, and follow-up before a safe discharge.

Our 24/7 laboratory, imaging, and pharmacy support all NICU diagnostic and treatment needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU)?
A Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) is a specialised hospital unit that provides round-the-clock care for premature, low-birth-weight, and critically ill newborns who need close monitoring and advanced medical support beyond routine newborn care.
Which newborns are admitted to the NICU at Ibn Sina Hospital?
Newborns who are premature, have low birth weight, breathing difficulties, jaundice requiring treatment, infections, or other conditions requiring continuous monitoring and specialised care are admitted to our NICU.
Is the NICU at Ibn Sina Hospital available 24/7?
Yes, our NICU operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with continuous monitoring and emergency response for newborns who need critical care.
Can parents stay with their baby in the NICU?
We encourage parental involvement in newborn care wherever medically appropriate and provide guidance and support to families throughout their baby's stay in the NICU.
What equipment and support does the NICU have?
Our NICU is equipped with incubators, radiant warmers, phototherapy units, continuous cardio-respiratory monitoring, and access to 24/7 laboratory, imaging, and pharmacy support for comprehensive newborn care.
