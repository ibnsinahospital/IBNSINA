What We Treat

  • Rheumatoid arthritis (chronic joint inflammation, pain, and stiffness)
  • Osteoarthritis (wear-and-tear arthritis affecting knees, hips, and hands)
  • Gout and other crystal-related joint diseases
  • Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) – an autoimmune disease affecting skin, joints, and organs
  • Ankylosing spondylitis (inflammatory arthritis of the spine and sacroiliac joints)

  • Scleroderma and Sjogren's syndrome (autoimmune connective tissue disorders)
  • Vasculitis and other rare inflammatory conditions

What to Expect

1. Consultation

Your journey begins with a thorough, unhurried consultation with Dr. Zubair. We take a detailed history of your symptoms – joint pain, morning stiffness, swelling, fatigue, skin rashes, or any other concerns. We also review your family history and any previous test results to understand the full picture of your health.

2. Diagnosis

Accurate diagnosis is the cornerstone of rheumatology. We use advanced diagnostic tools available on-site at Ibn Sina Hospital, including specialised blood tests (like rheumatoid factor, anti-CCP, ANA, ESR, and CRP) and advanced imaging such as X-rays, ultrasound, and MRI to assess joint damage, inflammation, and soft tissue involvement. Our in-house laboratory ensures fast, reliable results.

3. Treatment

Your treatment is personalised to your specific condition and its severity. It may include disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs (DMARDs), biologic therapies, corticosteroids, pain relievers, and physiotherapy. We aim not just to relieve pain but to slow disease progression, preserve joint function, and improve your overall wellbeing. For patients with lupus or vasculitis, we also manage multi-organ involvement with careful monitoring.

4. Follow-up & Ongoing Care

Rheumatological conditions are often long-term. We schedule regular follow-up appointments to monitor disease activity, adjust medications, and track your response to treatment. Our goal is to keep you in remission, minimise flares, and help you maintain an active, fulfilling life. We are always available to address any concerns between visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book an appointment with Dr. Zubair at Ibn Sina Hospital?

You can book an appointment by calling our hospital reception, visiting us in person, or using our online appointment system. Our friendly staff will help you schedule a convenient time for your consultation.

What blood tests are needed for rheumatological conditions?

Depending on your symptoms, we may recommend tests like rheumatoid factor, anti-CCP, ANA (antinuclear antibody), ESR, CRP, and complete blood counts. These help us identify autoimmune activity and inflammation. All tests are performed in our in-house laboratory with quick results.

Do you offer biologic therapies and infusion treatments for rheumatoid arthritis and lupus?

Yes. We provide advanced disease-modifying therapies, including biologic agents and targeted synthetic DMARDs. For patients who require intravenous infusions, we have a comfortable, monitored infusion suite where you can receive treatment in a safe, relaxed environment.

Does health insurance cover rheumatology consultations, tests, and biologic medications?

We accept most major health insurance policies and offer cashless treatment for eligible patients. Many policies cover rheumatology consultations, diagnostic tests, and even biologic therapies. Our billing team will assist you with paperwork and pre-authorisation. Please bring your insurance card and valid ID at the time of your visit.

What should I do if I experience a sudden, severe joint flare or medication reaction after hours?

Ibn Sina Hospital has a 24/7 emergency department with a consultant available at all times. If you experience sudden, unbearable joint pain, severe swelling, or signs of an allergic reaction to medication, come directly to the hospital or call our emergency helpline. We are always ready to provide immediate, expert care.
