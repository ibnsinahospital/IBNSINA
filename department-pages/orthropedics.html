What to Expect

1. Consultation

Your journey begins with a thorough one-on-one consultation with one of our orthopaedic surgeons. We take a detailed history of your pain, injury, or limitation, and we listen carefully to your goals – whether you want to walk without a limp, play cricket again, or simply get through your day without discomfort.

2. Diagnosis

We use state-of-the-art imaging facilities available right here at Ibn Sina Hospital, including digital X-ray, ultrasound, and advanced MRI scans. Our radiologists work closely with the orthopaedic team to pinpoint the exact cause of your problem, so we never treat blindly.

3. Treatment

Your treatment is tailor-made for you. It may range from physiotherapy and medication to minimally invasive arthroscopic surgery or full joint replacement. For patients needing surgery, we use modern techniques that minimise tissue damage, reduce hospital stays, and speed up recovery. Our joint replacement surgeries use high-quality implants designed for long-lasting function.

4. Follow-up & Rehabilitation

Recovery does not end in the operating theatre. We provide a structured rehabilitation plan, including on-site physiotherapy, to help you regain strength, flexibility, and confidence. Our team monitors your progress closely and is always available to answer your questions during follow-up visits.

Facilities

  • In-house physiotherapy and rehabilitation centre with experienced therapists

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book an appointment with an orthopaedic surgeon at Ibn Sina Hospital?

You can book an appointment by calling our hospital reception, visiting us in person, or using our online appointment system. Our staff will help you schedule a convenient time with the surgeon best suited to your condition.

Do you offer joint replacement surgery for elderly patients with arthritis?

Yes. We specialise in total knee replacement, hip replacement, and shoulder replacement surgeries, particularly for elderly patients suffering from severe arthritis. Our team carefully evaluates each patient’s fitness for surgery and provides comprehensive pre- and post-operative care.

Does my health insurance cover orthopaedic surgery and joint replacement?

We accept most major health insurance policies and offer cashless treatment for eligible patients. Please bring your insurance card and valid identification at the time of admission, and our billing department will guide you through the entire process.

Is physiotherapy available after surgery or injury?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated physiotherapy department with qualified orthopaedic physiotherapists who work with you from day one – whether you have had a joint replacement, a fracture, or a sports injury. Rehabilitation is a key part of your recovery, and we ensure you have all the support you need.

What should I do if I have an orthopaedic emergency – like a broken bone or severe joint injury – after hours?

Ibn Sina Hospital has a fully functional 24/7 emergency department with an on-call orthopaedic surgeon available at all times. If you experience a fracture, dislocation, or any severe bone or joint injury, come directly to the hospital or call our emergency helpline. We are always ready to provide immediate, life-saving care.
