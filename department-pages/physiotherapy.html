What to Expect – Your Rehabilitation Journey

1

Comprehensive Assessment

Our physiotherapist performs a thorough evaluation of your condition, including strength, range of motion, balance, and functional limitations to create a personalized treatment plan.

2

Personalised Treatment Plan

We develop a customized rehabilitation program including therapeutic exercises, manual therapy, electrotherapy, and patient education to meet your specific goals.

3

Active Therapy & Monitoring

You will participate in supervised therapy sessions, with regular progress assessments and adjustments to your treatment plan for optimal recovery.

4

Ongoing Support & Prevention

Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does physiotherapy treat?
Physiotherapy treats a wide range of conditions including musculoskeletal pain (back, neck, joint), sports injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation, stroke rehabilitation, neurological disorders (Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis), and chronic pain conditions.
Do you offer orthopaedic and neuro physiotherapy?
Yes, we have both orthopaedic physiotherapists for joint and muscle conditions, and neuro physiotherapists for stroke, spinal cord injury, and neurological rehabilitation. We provide specialized care for each patient's needs.
What is the recovery time with physiotherapy?
Recovery time varies depending on the condition. Acute injuries may improve in 4-6 weeks, while chronic conditions may require 3-6 months of regular therapy. Our physiotherapists will provide a personalized timeline during your first visit.
Do you treat sports injuries?
Yes, we offer sports injury assessment and rehabilitation including ligament sprains, muscle strains, tendonitis, and overuse injuries. Our therapists work with athletes to ensure safe return to sport.
Is physiotherapy covered by insurance?
Yes, most insurance plans cover physiotherapy when prescribed by a doctor. We can help you understand your coverage and submit claims on your behalf.
