What to Expect

1. Consultation

Your journey begins with a private, unhurried consultation with one of our gastroenterologists. We take a detailed history of your symptoms – whether it is chronic heartburn, abdominal pain, bloating, diarrhoea, or jaundice. We also review your diet, lifestyle, and any family history of digestive diseases.

2. Diagnosis

We use advanced diagnostic tools available on-site at Ibn Sina Hospital, including abdominal ultrasound, upper GI endoscopy (gastroscopy), colonoscopy, and specialised liver function tests. Our endoscopy suite is equipped with high-definition cameras for clear, accurate visualisation. For complex cases, we offer CT and MRI imaging.

3. Treatment

Your treatment is personalised to your condition. It may include dietary advice, medications to reduce acid or control inflammation, and management of chronic liver disease. For patients needing interventions, we offer therapeutic endoscopy – including polypectomy, variceal banding, stricture dilation, and ERCP for gallstone removal and pancreatic drainage.

4. Follow-up

Digestive and liver conditions often require ongoing monitoring. We schedule regular follow-ups to assess your response to treatment, adjust medications, and perform repeat endoscopies or imaging when needed. Our goal is to keep you symptom-free and prevent long-term complications.

Facilities

  • State-of-the-art endoscopy suite with high-definition gastroscopes and colonoscopes
  • Therapeutic ERCP (Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography) facility for pancreatic and biliary interventions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book an appointment with a gastroenterologist at Ibn Sina Hospital?

You can book an appointment by calling our hospital reception, visiting us in person, or using our online appointment portal. Our staff will help you schedule a convenient time with the specialist best suited to your condition.

Do you offer endoscopy and colonoscopy services? Are they painful?

Yes. We have a fully equipped endoscopy suite offering both diagnostic and therapeutic gastroscopy and colonoscopy. All procedures are performed under sedation, so you remain comfortable and pain-free throughout. Our team explains the procedure in detail beforehand so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you treat hepatitis B and C? What about fatty liver disease?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated liver clinic that manages viral hepatitis (B and C) with the latest antiviral therapies, as well as non-alcoholic and alcoholic fatty liver disease through lifestyle modifications, dietary counselling, and medical management. We also monitor for cirrhosis and liver cancer with regular imaging and blood tests.

Does health insurance cover gastroenterology consultations, endoscopies, and treatments?

We accept most major health insurance policies and offer cashless treatment for eligible patients. Endoscopies, colonoscopies, and other gastroenterology procedures are typically covered. Our billing team will help you with all necessary paperwork and pre-authorisation. Please bring your insurance card and valid ID at the time of your visit.

What should I do if I experience severe abdominal pain, vomiting blood, or black, tarry stools after hours?

These could be signs of a gastrointestinal emergency, such as a bleeding ulcer or variceal rupture. Ibn Sina Hospital has a 24/7 emergency department with a gastroenterologist on call at all times. Do not delay – come directly to the hospital or call our emergency helpline immediately. Prompt endoscopy can be life-saving.
