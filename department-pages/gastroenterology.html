Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does a gastroenterologist treat?
Gastroenterologists treat conditions affecting the digestive system including acid reflux, GERD, ulcers, irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), liver diseases (hepatitis, cirrhosis), pancreatitis, gallbladder disorders, and gastrointestinal cancers.
What are the symptoms of acid reflux and GERD?
Common symptoms include heartburn (burning sensation in the chest), regurgitation of food or sour liquid, difficulty swallowing, chest pain, and a sensation of a lump in your throat. If these occur more than twice a week, you may have GERD.
Do you offer endoscopy and colonoscopy services?
Yes, we provide comprehensive diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy services including upper GI endoscopy (EGD), colonoscopy, ERCP, and capsule endoscopy. Our advanced endoscopy suite ensures accurate diagnosis and minimally invasive treatment.
What is the treatment for fatty liver disease?
Treatment for non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) includes lifestyle modifications such as weight loss, healthy diet, exercise, and managing associated conditions like diabetes and high cholesterol. Our gastroenterologist will create a personalized management plan.
When should I see a gastroenterologist for digestive issues?
You should see a gastroenterologist if you experience persistent abdominal pain, chronic heartburn, difficulty swallowing, unexplained weight loss, blood in stool, changes in bowel habits, or any other concerning digestive symptoms that last more than a few weeks.
