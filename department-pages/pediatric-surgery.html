Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does a pediatric surgeon treat?
Pediatric surgeons treat a wide range of surgical conditions in children including congenital anomalies (e.g., hernias, undescended testis, intestinal atresia), appendicitis, trauma, tumours, and gastrointestinal disorders in newborns, infants, and children.
When should a child see a pediatric surgeon?
A child may need to see a pediatric surgeon if they have a condition that requires surgical intervention, such as persistent abdominal pain, a lump or swelling, undescended testicle, hernia, or if they have had a traumatic injury requiring surgical care.
What is the difference between a pediatric surgeon and a general surgeon?
Pediatric surgeons are specially trained to perform surgery on children, from newborns to adolescents. They have expertise in the unique anatomy and physiology of growing children and use age-appropriate techniques and equipment to ensure the best outcomes.
Does Ibn Sina Hospital offer minimally invasive pediatric surgery?
Yes, we offer advanced laparoscopic and minimally invasive techniques for pediatric patients, including appendectomy, hernia repair, and other procedures. Minimally invasive surgery results in less pain, smaller scars, and faster recovery for children.
What emergency pediatric surgical services are available?
We provide 24/7 emergency surgical care for children with acute conditions such as appendicitis, bowel obstruction, trauma, and other life-threatening emergencies. Our pediatric surgical team is always on standby to provide prompt and expert care.
