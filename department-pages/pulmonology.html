Frequently Asked Questions

What respiratory conditions does a pulmonologist treat?
Pulmonologists treat a wide range of respiratory conditions including asthma, COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease), pneumonia, bronchitis, tuberculosis, interstitial lung disease, pulmonary fibrosis, lung cancer, sleep apnoea, and respiratory infections.
What are the symptoms of COPD?
Common symptoms of COPD include chronic cough, shortness of breath (especially during physical activity), wheezing, chest tightness, frequent respiratory infections, and fatigue. If you experience these, consult our pulmonologist.
Do you offer pulmonary function testing?
Yes, we provide comprehensive pulmonary function tests (PFTs) including spirometry, lung volume measurement, diffusing capacity testing, and bronchial challenge tests to accurately assess lung function and diagnose respiratory conditions.
What is the treatment for asthma?
Asthma treatment includes inhaled corticosteroids, bronchodilators, oral medications, and allergen avoidance. We also provide asthma education, action plans, and regular monitoring to help you manage your symptoms effectively.
Do you treat sleep apnoea?
Yes, our pulmonology department provides evaluation and treatment for sleep apnoea including sleep studies (polysomnography), CPAP therapy, oral appliances, and lifestyle modifications. Our team helps you achieve better sleep and improved quality of life.
