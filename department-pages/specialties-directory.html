Medical Departments & Specialties

Ibn Sina Hospital in Budgam provides access to multiple medical and surgical specialties for patients from Budgam and across the Kashmir Valley and Jammu & Kashmir. Select a department to learn more about the specialty and available care.

Clinical & Surgical Specialties

  • Gynaecology — women's health, obstetric and gynaecological care.
  • Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement — bone, joint, trauma and joint replacement care.
  • Urology — urinary tract, prostate, stone and urological surgical care.
  • ENT — ear, nose, throat and head-and-neck care.
  • General Surgery — surgical assessment and general surgical procedures.
  • General Medicine — adult medical assessment and management of common and chronic conditions.
  • Cardiology — heart and cardiovascular assessment and specialist care.
  • Nephrology — kidney disease and renal care.
  • Pediatric Surgery — surgical care for children.
  • Gastroenterology — digestive and liver health, including specialist evaluation.
  • Dentistry — dental and oral healthcare.
  • Ophthalmology — eye and vision care.
  • Plastic Surgery — reconstructive and plastic surgical care.
  • Dermatology — skin, hair and related dermatological care.
  • Pulmonology — respiratory and lung care.
  • Rheumatology — arthritis, autoimmune and musculoskeletal conditions.

Diagnostic, Rehabilitation & Critical Care Services

  • Physiotherapy — rehabilitation and musculoskeletal/neuro physiotherapy.
  • Radiology — diagnostic imaging services.
  • Neonatal Intensive Care Unit — specialist neonatal care.
