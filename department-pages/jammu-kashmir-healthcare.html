Multi-Specialty Healthcare in Jammu & Kashmir

Ibn Sina Hospital is a multi-specialty hospital in Budgam, Jammu & Kashmir. From our Budgam location, we provide consultant-led medical care and hospital services for patients from Budgam, the Kashmir Valley and other parts of Jammu & Kashmir.

Our aim is to make specialist consultation, diagnostics, emergency support and essential hospital services accessible in one place, while connecting patients with the appropriate department and doctor for their needs.

Hospital Services

Depending on clinical need and availability, patients can access hospital services including 24/7 OPD and emergency support, pharmacy, laboratory services, dialysis, endoscopy, digital X-ray, TMT, Holter monitoring and ABPM.
