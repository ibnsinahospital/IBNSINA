Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does a general medicine specialist treat?
General medicine specialists (internists) treat a wide range of adult conditions including hypertension, diabetes, respiratory infections, gastrointestinal disorders, thyroid problems, cardiovascular diseases, and chronic conditions like COPD and asthma.
When should I see a general medicine doctor?
You should see a general medicine doctor for routine check-ups, management of chronic conditions, unexplained symptoms, persistent fever, cough, abdominal pain, or any health concern that requires comprehensive medical evaluation and treatment.
Does Ibn Sina Hospital offer preventive health check-ups?
Yes, we offer comprehensive preventive health check-up packages for adults including blood tests, ECG, chest X-ray, and specialist consultations to detect and manage health risks early.
What diagnostic services are available for general medicine patients?
We provide a full range of diagnostic services including laboratory tests (blood, urine, stool), imaging (X-ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI), ECG, echocardiography, pulmonary function tests, and endoscopy.
Do you manage diabetes and hypertension at Ibn Sina Hospital?
Yes, we provide comprehensive management for diabetes and hypertension including medication, lifestyle counselling, regular monitoring, and education to help patients control these chronic conditions effectively.
