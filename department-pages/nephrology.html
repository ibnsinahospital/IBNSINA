What We Treat

  • Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) – gradual loss of kidney function
  • Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) – sudden kidney failure due to infection, dehydration, or medications
  • Kidney stones and recurrent urinary tract infections affecting the kidneys
  • Glomerulonephritis – inflammation of the kidney's filtering units
  • Diabetic nephropathy – kidney damage caused by diabetes (common in J&K)
  • Hypertensive kidney disease – high blood pressure damaging the kidneys
  • End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) requiring dialysis or transplant evaluation
  • Electrolyte imbalances and acid-base disorders
  • Fluid overload and oedema management

What to Expect

1. Consultation

Your journey begins with a detailed, unhurried consultation with Dr. Aijaz Arshid. We take a complete medical history, discuss your symptoms (swelling, fatigue, changes in urine, high blood pressure), and review any underlying conditions like diabetes or hypertension. We listen carefully to all your concerns and answer every question.

2. Diagnosis

We use advanced diagnostic tools available on-site at Ibn Sina Hospital, including blood tests (creatinine, urea, electrolytes, eGFR), urine analysis, kidney ultrasound, and, when needed, advanced imaging or kidney biopsy. Our in-house laboratory ensures rapid, accurate results so we can start treatment without delay.

3. Treatment

Your treatment is tailored to your specific condition. It may include lifestyle changes, dietary modifications (salt, protein, and potassium control), medications to control blood pressure and blood sugar, and management of anaemia and bone health. For patients with advanced kidney failure, we offer haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis, as well as pre-transplant evaluation and post-transplant care.

4. Follow-up & Long-Term Care

Kidney disease often requires lifelong management. We schedule regular follow-up appointments to monitor your kidney function, adjust medications, and track your overall health. Our goal is to slow disease progression, prevent complications, and ensure you live as actively and comfortably as possible. We are always available for urgent concerns between visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book an appointment with Dr. Aijaz Arshid at Ibn Sina Hospital?

You can book an appointment by calling our hospital reception, visiting us in person, or using our online booking system. Our staff will help you schedule a convenient time for your consultation.

Do you offer dialysis services? How does it work?

Yes. We have a fully equipped haemodialysis unit with modern machines and trained nursing staff. Dialysis is a treatment that filters waste and excess fluid from your blood when your kidneys can no longer do so. Our team will assess your condition and discuss whether you need short-term or long-term dialysis, and we guide you through every step.

Do you treat diabetic kidney disease?

Absolutely. Diabetic nephropathy is one of our key focus areas. We work closely with our internal medicine and endocrinology teams to manage blood sugar, blood pressure, and kidney function – aiming to slow or prevent progression to kidney failure.

Does health insurance cover nephrology consultations, dialysis, and kidney transplant evaluation?

We accept most major health insurance policies and offer cashless treatment for eligible patients. Many policies cover dialysis and transplant-related expenses. Our billing team will assist you with all necessary paperwork and pre-authorisation. Please bring your insurance card and valid ID at the time of your visit.

What should I do if I experience sudden swelling, breathlessness, or very low urine output after hours?

These could be signs of acute kidney injury or fluid overload – both of which require urgent attention. Ibn Sina Hospital has a 24/7 emergency department with a nephrologist on call at all times. Come directly to the hospital or call our emergency helpline immediately. Prompt care can prevent serious complications.
