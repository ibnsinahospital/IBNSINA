Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does a nephrologist treat?
Nephrologists treat all kidney-related conditions including chronic kidney disease (CKD), acute kidney injury (AKI), kidney stones, glomerulonephritis, diabetic nephropathy, hypertension, electrolyte imbalances, and end-stage renal disease requiring dialysis or transplant.
What are the early warning signs of kidney disease?
Early warning signs include swelling in ankles, feet or hands, fatigue, foamy or bloody urine, changes in urination frequency, persistent itching, nausea, and difficulty concentrating. If you experience these, consult our nephrologist.
Do you offer dialysis services at Ibn Sina Hospital?
Yes, we provide both haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis services in our state-of-the-art dialysis unit. Our team of trained nephrologists and nursing staff ensure safe and comfortable dialysis treatments.
What is diabetic nephropathy and how is it managed?
Diabetic nephropathy is kidney damage caused by diabetes. Management includes strict blood sugar control, blood pressure management, medication (ACE inhibitors/ARBs), dietary changes, and regular monitoring of kidney function to slow progression.
How can I prevent kidney stones?
Prevention includes drinking plenty of water (2-3 litres daily), reducing salt intake, limiting oxalate-rich foods (spinach, nuts), maintaining a healthy weight, and following a balanced diet. Our nephrologist can provide personalised dietary advice.
