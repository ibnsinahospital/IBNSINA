Frequently Asked Questions

What are the common symptoms of cataracts?
Common symptoms include blurred or cloudy vision, difficulty seeing at night, sensitivity to light, halos around lights, fading of colours, and frequent changes in prescription glasses. If you experience these, consult our ophthalmologist.
When should I get my eyes checked?
We recommend a comprehensive eye exam every 1-2 years for adults, and annually for those over 60 or with risk factors like diabetes, high blood pressure, or a family history of eye disease.
What is glaucoma and how is it treated?
Glaucoma is a group of eye conditions that damage the optic nerve, often due to high pressure in the eye. Treatment includes prescription eye drops, laser therapy, or surgery to lower pressure and prevent further vision loss.
Do you offer LASIK or refractive surgery?
Yes, our ophthalmology department provides advanced refractive surgery including LASIK and PRK for vision correction. Our specialists will assess your suitability and discuss the best option for you.
What facilities are available for eye emergencies?
We provide 24/7 emergency eye care for conditions like retinal detachment, acute glaucoma, foreign body injuries, and infections. Our emergency team is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and is always on standby.
