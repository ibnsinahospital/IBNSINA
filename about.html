About Ibn Sina Hospital

Founded in 2018, Ibn Sina Hospital has grown into Budgam's most trusted multi-specialty hospital — a place where comprehensive care meets genuine compassion. Our motto, “High Quality Facilities at Affordable Costs,” drives every decision we make.

We follow a consultant-led care model: experienced specialists oversee treatment plans from diagnosis through recovery, ensuring that every patient receives expert-guided, personalized attention. From preventive check‑ups to complex surgeries, our team delivers a seamless healthcare experience under one roof.

Why Choose Ibn Sina Hospital

Consultant‑Led Care

Senior consultants direct every case, not just on paper but actively at the bedside.

Dedicated Team

Nurses, technicians, and support staff trained in empathy and excellence.

Same‑Day Diagnostics

Digital X‑rays, ultrasound, and lab results often available within hours.

State‑of‑the‑Art Facilities

Modern operation theatres, dialysis unit, endoscopy suite, and advanced imaging.

24/7 Pharmacy & Lab

We never close — emergency medications and tests anytime, any day.

Affordable & Accessible

High-quality care priced fairly, serving the community without compromise.
