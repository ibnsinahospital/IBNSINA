📋 How to Book an Appointment

Choose the method that works best for you. We're here to make booking easy.

🇬🇧 English EN

  1. Fill in the form – Enter your name, phone number, and preferred department or doctor.
  2. Choose your date – Pick a preferred date (optional) and add any special notes.
  3. Submit your request – Click "Request Appointment" and our team will receive your details.
  4. Wait for confirmation – We'll call you within 24 hours to confirm your appointment.
💡 Or call us directly: 9622552553 / 9419023501
