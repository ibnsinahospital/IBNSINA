Dr. Wasim

Specialty: Gastroenterologist

Department: GASTROENTEROLOGY

Qualifications: MBBS, MD (Internal Medicine), DNB (Gastroenterology)

About:
Dr. Wasim is a Consultant Gastroenterologist and Therapeutic Endoscopist, holding MBBS, MD (Internal Medicine), and DNB (Gastroenterology). He focuses on liver diseases, pancreaticobiliary disorders, gallstones, and pancreatitis. His expertise lies in advanced endoscopic procedures such as ERCP, stenting, and biopsies. He manages critical cases like GI bleeding, acute liver failure, and hepatic encephalopathy.
