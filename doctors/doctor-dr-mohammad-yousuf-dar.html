About:
Dr. Dar is a Consultant General Surgeon with qualifications in MBBS and MS General Surgery. He specializes in laparoscopic gallbladder and appendix surgery, hernia repair, and advanced gastrointestinal procedures. His work often involves emergency trauma care, including complex laparotomies and life-saving interventions for perforations, intestinal obstructions, and massive GI bleeds.
