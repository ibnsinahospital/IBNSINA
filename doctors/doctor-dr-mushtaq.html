About:
Dr. Mushtaq is a Consultant Plastic Surgeon with MBBS, MS, and MLC qualifications. He provides both reconstructive and cosmetic surgery services. His practice covers burn management, trauma reconstruction, cleft lip/palate repair, skin grafting, scar revision, and congenital deformity correction. He also performs cosmetic facial and body procedures, including breast reconstruction and body contouring.
