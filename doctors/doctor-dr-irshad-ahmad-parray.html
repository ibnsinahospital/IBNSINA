About:
Dr. Irshad Ahmad Parray is a Consultant Gastroenterologist at Ibn Sina Hospital, Budgam, specializing in comprehensive digestive and liver health. He holds an MBBS, MD (Medicine) from SKIMS, and a DNB and DM (Gastroenterology) from GMC Srinagar. Dr. Parray combines advanced diagnostics, including endoscopy and colonoscopy, with a compassionate approach to deliver expert care for a wide range of gastrointestinal and hepatic conditions.
