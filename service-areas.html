Where We Serve

Ibn Sina Hospital is located in Ompora, Budgam, near the Ompora Railway Station. We provide consultant-led healthcare for patients from across Budgam district and welcome patients from other parts of Jammu & Kashmir. Our 24/7 OPD, pharmacy, laboratory, and emergency services are available to anyone who needs them.

📅 Book an appointment · 🩺 View our services · 📍 Get directions

🏥 Budgam District — Our Primary Service Area Tier 1 · Priority

Our hospital is physically located in Ompora, Budgam, so patients from across Budgam district are our nearest and most natural community. If you live in or near any of the areas below, Ibn Sina Hospital is likely one of the closest multi-specialty hospitals to your home. Learn more about our hospital.

  • Ompora
  • Budgam
  • Chadoora
  • Beerwah
  • Khansahib
  • Charar-i-Sharief
  • Nearby Budgam areas

🏙️ Srinagar & Nearby Districts Tier 2 · Secondary

Budgam shares a border with Srinagar and is well-connected to several surrounding districts. Patients who prefer a smaller, quieter hospital environment — or who are looking for a specific specialist or service — may find Ibn Sina Hospital a suitable option. Explore our doctors and specialists or browse our services.

  • Srinagar
  • Pulwama
  • Ganderbal
  • Baramulla

🏔️ Wider Kashmir Valley Tier 3 · Regional

We also welcome patients from other districts of the Kashmir Valley. If you are seeking a specific speciality or service — such as nephrology, cardiology, or orthopaedics — our team is here to help.

  • Shopian
  • Anantnag
  • Kulgam
  • Kupwara
  • Bandipora

🌏 Wider Jammu & Kashmir Tier 4 · Broader

We also welcome patients from other parts of Jammu & Kashmir who are visiting Budgam, Srinagar, or the Kashmir Valley. If you have a specific consultation or procedure in mind, please contact us to confirm availability before travelling. You can also read more about healthcare at Ibn Sina Hospital for J&K patients.
