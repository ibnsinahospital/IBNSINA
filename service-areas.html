Serving All 20 Districts of Jammu & Kashmir

Ibn Sina Hospital in Ompora, Budgam is proud to serve patients from across the entire Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir. From the Kashmir Valley to the Jammu Region and Ladakh, patients trust us for quality healthcare that is affordable and accessible.

📍 Districts we serve: Budgam, Srinagar, Ganderbal, Pulwama, Shopian, Kulgam, Baramulla, Anantnag, Kupwara, Bandipora, Jammu, Kathua, Samba, Udhampur, Rajouri, Poonch, Doda, Kishtwar, Kargil, Leh
