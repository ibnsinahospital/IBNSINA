🏥 General Questions

Where is Ibn Sina Hospital located?

Ibn Sina Hospital is located near the Railway Station, Ompora Railway Station Road, Ompora, Budgam, Jammu & Kashmir – 191111. View on map →

What are the OPD timings at Ibn Sina Hospital?

Our OPD is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. We never close – you can visit us anytime for medical consultations and emergency care.

Does Ibn Sina Hospital treat patients from outside Budgam?

Yes, we welcome patients from across Jammu and Kashmir, including Srinagar, Baramulla, Pulwama, Ganderbal, Shopian, Anantnag, and also treat patients visiting from other parts of India.

What languages are spoken at the hospital?

Our staff speak Kashmiri, Urdu, Hindi, and English to ensure clear communication with all patients.

What makes Ibn Sina Hospital different from other hospitals in Budgam?

Ibn Sina Hospital combines a 24/7 in-house pharmacy, round-the-clock lab services, and multiple specialties (cardiology, nephrology, orthopedics, gynaecology, physiotherapy, dialysis, ambulance, diagnostics) all under one roof – with experienced consultants and affordable care.

🩺 Services

What medical specialties are available at Ibn Sina Hospital?

We offer over 20 specialties including Cardiology, Nephrology, Orthopedics, Gynaecology, Urology, Pediatrics, Gastroenterology, General Surgery, ENT, Ophthalmology, Dermatology, Pulmonology, Rheumatology, Physiotherapy, and more. View all services →

Does the hospital provide dialysis treatment?

Yes, dialysis services are available for patients with kidney failure. Patients should call 9622552553 to check availability and scheduling.

Does the hospital provide physiotherapy?

Yes. We offer physiotherapy services covering injury prevention, rehabilitation, and management of acute and chronic conditions.

Does Ibn Sina Hospital provide endoscopy services?

Yes, we offer advanced upper and lower GI endoscopy including colonoscopy for accurate internal diagnosis.

Does the hospital perform TMT (Treadmill Test)?

Yes, we have a Cardiac Stress Testing (TMT) lab for heart health assessment under expert supervision.

Does Ibn Sina Hospital offer Holter Monitoring and ABPM?

Yes, we provide 24-hour Holter Monitoring for ECG recording and Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring (ABPM) to assess hypertension patterns.

Can I get a digital X-ray at Ibn Sina Hospital?

Yes, we have high-resolution digital radiography with same-day results for fast, accurate diagnosis.

Does the hospital provide maternity services?

Yes, our Gynaecology department provides comprehensive maternity services including antenatal care, delivery, postnatal care, and women's health services. View Gynaecology Department →

Does Ibn Sina Hospital have pediatric services?

Yes, we have pediatric services including pediatric surgery and general pediatric care for children of all ages.

🏗️ Facilities

Is the pharmacy open 24/7?

Yes. Ibn Sina Pharmacy operates 365 days a year, 24 hours a day — the only pharmacy of its kind in Budgam that never closes.

Are laboratory/diagnostic services available round the clock?

Yes, the lab provides round-the-clock service for inpatients and outpatients, with fast, accurate turnaround times.

Does the hospital have a 24/7 emergency room?

Yes, our emergency room is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. We have experienced doctors and staff ready to handle all medical emergencies.

Is parking available at the hospital?

Yes, we have ample parking space available on the hospital premises for patients and visitors.

Visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. However, for ICU and critical care patients, visiting is limited and requires prior approval from the attending doctor.
