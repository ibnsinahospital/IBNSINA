Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ibn Sina Hospital located?

Ibn Sina Hospital is located near the Railway Station, Ompora Railway Station Road, Ompora, Budgam, Jammu & Kashmir – 191111.

What services does Ibn Sina Hospital offer?

The hospital offers multispeciality services including Laboratory diagnostics, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Sigmoidoscopy, Dialysis, and Ambulance/Emergency services.

Is the pharmacy open 24/7?

Yes. Ibn Sina Pharmacy operates 365 days a year, 24 hours a day — the only pharmacy of its kind in Budgam that never closes.

Are laboratory/diagnostic services available round the clock?

Yes, the lab provides round-the-clock service for inpatients and outpatients, with fast, accurate turnaround times.

Does the hospital offer dialysis treatment?

Yes, dialysis services are available for patients with kidney failure. Patients should call to check availability and scheduling.

Is ambulance service available in an emergency?

Yes, the hospital runs an ambulance service as part of its Emergency Medicine department.

Does the hospital provide physiotherapy?

Yes. Physiotherapy covers injury prevention, rehabilitation, and management of acute and chronic conditions.

How do I contact the hospital or book an appointment?

Reception: 9622552553 / 9419023501. Administration: 9622392553 / 9149606115 / 7006272634. Email: weibnsina@gmail.com.

What is Sigmoidoscopy, and is it available here?

Yes, the hospital performs sigmoidoscopy — a procedure using a flexible tube with a light to examine the sigmoid colon.

What makes Ibn Sina Hospital different from other hospitals in Budgam?

It combines a 24/7 in-house pharmacy, round-the-clock lab services, and multiple specialities (physiotherapy, dialysis, ambulance, diagnostics) under one roof.
