Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ibn Sina Hospital located?

Near Railway Station, Ompora Railway Station Road, Ompora, Budgam, J&K 191111.

Does Ibn Sina Hospital serve patients outside Budgam?

Yes, we welcome and treat patients from across Jammu and Kashmir, including Srinagar, Baramulla, Pulwama, Anantnag, Ganderbal, Shopian and Kulgam.

Is the pharmacy open 24/7?

Yes, Ibn Sina Pharmacy is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Does the hospital provide dialysis?

Yes, haemodialysis services are available at our nephrology and dialysis unit.

How do I book an appointment?

Call reception on 9622552553 or 9419023501, or use our online appointment booking page.

Is ambulance service available?

Yes, Ibn Sina Hospital operates an ambulance service alongside a 24/7 emergency department.
