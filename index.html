Our Core Services

Ambulance Services

24/7 emergency ambulance service for transporting patients to and from the hospital.

Endoscopy

Advanced upper and lower GI endoscopy including colonoscopy for accurate internal diagnosis.

Dialysis

In-house dialysis unit providing life-sustaining renal care with experienced nephrology support.

Digital X-Rays

High-resolution digital radiography with same-day results for fast, accurate diagnosis.

Vaccinations

Complete immunization services for children and adults — routine, travel, and seasonal vaccines.

TMT (Treadmill Test)

Cardiac stress testing for heart health assessment — conducted under expert supervision.

Holter Monitoring

Continuous 24-hour ECG recording to detect irregular heart rhythms that may not appear during a routine ECG.

ABPM (Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring)

24-hour blood pressure monitoring to assess hypertension patterns and adjust treatment accurately.

Ultrasonography

Detailed ultrasound imaging for abdominal, obstetric, vascular, and soft-tissue evaluation.

Colonoscopy

Thorough colonoscopic screening and diagnostic procedures for gastrointestinal health.

24/7 Pharmacy

In-house pharmacy — we never close. Emergency medications and prescriptions anytime.

24/7 Diagnostic Lab

Round-the-clock laboratory services for in-patients and out-patients, with rapid turnaround.
