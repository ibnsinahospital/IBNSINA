Frequently Asked Questions — Ganderbal Patients

Does Ibn Sina Hospital accept patients from Ganderbal?
Yes, we regularly welcome patients from Ganderbal district for OPD consultations, dialysis, diagnostics, and emergency care. Our team can guide you on travel and appointment scheduling by phone before you visit.
Is dialysis available for Ganderbal patients at Ibn Sina Hospital?
Yes, dialysis services are available at Ibn Sina Hospital, Budgam. We welcome dialysis patients from Ganderbal and surrounding areas and can discuss scheduling to fit ongoing treatment needs.
Is PM-JAY / Ayushman Bharat cashless treatment available for Ganderbal patients?
Yes, Ibn Sina Hospital is a PM-JAY empanelled hospital. Eligible patients from Ganderbal can avail cashless treatment under the Ayushman Bharat scheme. Visit our PM-JAY / Insurance page or call for eligibility details.
Do you have emergency and ambulance support for patients coming from Ganderbal?
Yes, our emergency department and ambulance service operate 24/7, 365 days a year. Call our emergency number directly for urgent situations.
