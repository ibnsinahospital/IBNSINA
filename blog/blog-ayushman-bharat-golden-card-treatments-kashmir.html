Ayushman Bharat (Golden Card) in Kashmir: Complete List of Cashless Surgeries & Treatments

Confused about what the PM-JAY SEHAT Golden Card covers in private hospitals? Here is every Kashmiri family's complete guide to cashless surgery, dialysis, and inpatient care at Ibn Sina Hospital, Budgam.

In Kashmir, a sudden medical emergency doesn't just affect one person. It affects the entire family. For decades, a major surgery, a kidney stone operation, or regular dialysis meant dipping into life savings, selling land, or borrowing money from relatives.

Then came the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY SEHAT Golden Card — a scheme designed to give every single resident of Jammu & Kashmir up to ₹5 lakh of free, cashless treatment per family every year.

But here is something every Kashmiri household needs to know.

When someone falls sick, most families still don't know which private hospitals accept the Golden Card, which surgeries are covered, and why some things (like routine OPD tests) are paid while inpatient surgeries are cashless. Because of this confusion, thousands of families across Srinagar, Budgam, and the valley still end up paying out of pocket — or waiting months in crowded hospital queues.

At Ibn Sina Hospital in Ompora, Budgam[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span), our Ayushman Mitra helpdesk[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span) helps families from across J&K get cashless treatment every single day. This guide explains everything clearly so you can use your Golden Card without stress when your family needs it most.

Why Families in Kashmir Still Struggle to Use Their Golden Card

Unlike many states where Ayushman Bharat is limited, the PM-JAY SEHAT scheme in Jammu & Kashmir is universal. Almost every resident family with a valid ration card or Aadhaar-linked record is eligible. Yet patients face problems during emergencies because of:

  • Lack of awareness about private hospitals — not knowing you can get specialist surgery in a clean private hospital under the same Golden Card
  • OPD vs. IPD confusion — misunderstanding why walk-in consultations aren't covered, while full admissions and surgeries are
  • Missing documents — arriving at the hospital without the patient's Aadhaar card, linked phone number, or diagnostic reports
  • Delayed care — postponing gallbladder, hernia, or stone surgery out of fear of heavy bills

The good news: knowing the exact rules before an emergency happens can save your family both time and tens of thousands of rupees.

How Cashless Treatment Works — Step by Step

Using your Golden Card at Ibn Sina Hospital does not involve running around government offices. Our in-house team handles the online process right at the counter:

Step 1: Specialist Consultation & Diagnosis

You meet the specialist at Ibn Sina Hospital (such as our General Surgeon, Urologist, or Nephrologist). The doctor examines the patient and reviews ultrasound, X-ray, or blood reports to confirm that surgery, dialysis, or inpatient admission is required.

Step 2: Verification at the Ayushman Mitra Desk

Once admission is advised, visit our dedicated Ayushman Mitra Desk inside the hospital[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span). Present the patient's Golden Card and Aadhaar Card for biometric thumbprint or OTP verification.

Step 3: Online Pre-Authorization

Step 4: Cashless Treatment & Discharge

Once approved, the patient undergoes the surgery, dialysis, or inpatient treatment under our senior consultants and is discharged without paying for the covered package procedure.

The Golden Rule: What Is Covered vs. Not Covered

Under official National Health Authority (NHA) guidelines across every hospital in Jammu & Kashmir:

  • Hospital Admissions (IPD) & Surgeries ARE COVERED — when a patient is admitted for an approved surgical procedure, inpatient medical management, or daycare treatment like dialysis.
  • Walk-in Outpatient Visits (OPD) ARE NOT COVERED — routine doctor consultations, walk-in blood tests, ultrasounds, X-rays, or endoscopies done without hospital admission are not covered by the Golden Card.
  • Prior Diagnostic Proof is Mandatory — to approve any surgery (like gallbladder or stone removal), the government portal requires clear ultrasound, CT, or lab proof of the condition before admission.

The pattern is simple: the Golden Card covers hospital admission, surgery, and dialysis — not routine walk-in checkups.

Warning Signs You Should Never Delay Treating

Because cashless surgery and dialysis are available right here in Budgam, never ignore symptoms that could turn into a life-threatening emergency:

Gallbladder, Appendix & Hernia Warning Signs

  • Sharp pain in the upper right stomach, especially after fatty meals[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)
  • Abdominal pain radiating to the right shoulder or back[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)
  • Repeated vomiting, severe bloating, or fever with chills[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)
  • Yellowing of the eyes or skin (jaundice)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)
  • A visible swelling or bulge in the groin or abdomen that hurts when coughing or lifting

Kidney Stone & Kidney Failure Warning Signs

  • Severe pain in the back or side (flank) moving toward the groin
  • Burning pain or blood while passing urine
  • Swelling in the feet, ankles, or puffiness around the eyes in the morning[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)
  • Extreme fatigue, nausea, and breathlessness (signs of declining kidney function)[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span)[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)

If you have already been advised surgery or dialysis on a report, delaying treatment only increases the risk of severe complications.

How to Prepare Before Coming to the Hospital

Follow these 6 simple steps to make sure your Golden Card approval happens smoothly without delay:

1. Bring Original Aadhaar Card & Golden Card

Always carry the patient's physical Golden Card (or a clear PDF on your phone) along with their original Aadhaar card.

2. Carry the Aadhaar-Linked Mobile Phone

For elderly patients whose thumbprints may fade, carrying the mobile phone linked to Aadhaar allows instant OTP verification at the counter.

3. Bring All Previous Ultrasound, Scan & Blood Reports

Do not leave old reports at home. The PM-JAY portal requires diagnostic reports to approve surgical packages immediately.

4. Check Name & Age Spelling

Ensure the patient's name and birth year match on both the Aadhaar card and Golden Card to prevent portal rejection.

5. Call Ahead for Planned Surgeries or Dialysis Slots

Before coming in for a planned gallbladder surgery, stone procedure, or dialysis session, call our reception at 9622552553[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span) so we can schedule your specialist and guide you on fasting.

6. Bring One Family Attendant

Have one responsible family member accompany the patient to complete helpdesk formalities while the patient rests comfortably.
